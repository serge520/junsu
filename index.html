Since October 2024, I have been a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Mathematics at The University of Hong Kong, working with Professor Guangyue Han.

My research is in information theory and probability, with a focus on feedback capacity and constructive coding for Gaussian channels with memory. I use methods from stochastic analysis, spectral factorization, and Hardy spaces.

Research

Questions, methods & constructions

I study how feedback changes the fundamental limits of communication, and how those limits can be approached through explicit coding schemes.

01 / CAPACITY

Gaussian feedback capacity

Variational characterizations for stationary Gaussian channels. Hardy-space methods, spectral factorization, and the role of independent input components.

Related work →
02 / CODING

Constructive feedback coding

Schalkwijk–Kailath coding for channels with autoregressive noise. Second-order message recursions, achievable rates, and comparisons with first-order schemes.

Related work →
03 / CONTINUOUS TIME

Continuous-time channels

Feedback communication with continuous-time Gaussian noise. ARMA models, stochastic analysis, and explicit capacity formulas.

  1. 2026

    A Hardy-Space Proof of the Filter-Only Gaussian Feedback-Capacity Formula

    Preprint

    Jun Su, Yang Xu, and Guangyue Han

    arXiv:2609.15977v2 · revised 25 Sep 2026

    arXiv ↗ PDF ↗
    Research summary

    We establish the filter-only feedback-capacity formula at the level of capacity suprema under the Paley–Wiener condition. Polynomial feedback filters suffice; neither rationality nor a positive essential lower bound on the noise spectrum is required. The argument does not assert attainment by a single filter.

  2. 2026

    Second-Order Schalkwijk–Kailath Coding for Autoregressive Gaussian Channels

    Preprint

    Jun Su, Guangyue Han, and Shlomo Shamai (Shitz)

    arXiv:2601.09329v4 · revised 23 Oct 2026

    arXiv ↗ PDF (v4) ↗
    Research summary

    We obtain an explicit rate formula for SK(2) on stationary AR(p) Gaussian channels. Under the same average-power budget, the best SK(2) rate is at least the best SK(1) rate; restricting the optimization to recursions whose two characteristic roots have modulus greater than one leaves the supremum unchanged. Capacity is attained for AWGN, AR(1), and an AR(2) subclass formed from two interleaved, independent AR(1) processes. For that subclass, genuinely second-order coding beats SK(1). Optimality for arbitrary AR(2) noise is not asserted.

  4. 2024

    Feedback Capacity of the Continuous-Time ARMA(1,1) Gaussian Channel

    Journal

    Jun Su, Guangyue Han, and Shlomo Shamai (Shitz)

    IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, 70(9), 6171–6188, 2024

    DOI ↗ arXiv ↗ PDF ↗
    Research summary

    We derive the feedback capacity of the continuous-time ARMA(1,1) Gaussian channel in closed form and characterize the benefit of feedback. The analysis also gives counterexamples to continuous-time analogues of the half-bit bound and Cover’s 2P conjecture.
